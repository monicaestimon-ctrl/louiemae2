Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

This policy explains how House of Louie Mae LLC, a Texas limited liability company based in Houston, Texas, doing business as Louie Mae ("we", "us"), collects, uses and protects information when you use louiemae.com, including our retail store and House of Louie Mae, our trade and commercial site at louiemae.com/trade.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, phone number, delivery address and order details. For a trade account, we also collect your business name, business type, state, website or portfolio link, professional credential, the last four digits of your EIN (never the full number), and a resale or tax-exempt certificate if you upload one. Partners may upload a W-9 for tax reporting.
  • Things you save: projects, boards, collages, inspiration images you upload or import from a Pinterest pin link, sourcing requests and client quotes.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number.
  • Usage information: basic device and browser information, pages visited, and, if you arrive through a partner link, the partner code and channel so the right partner is credited.

Signing in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us, and tells us whether Google has verified that email. We use this only to create and sign you in to your account and to match it with an existing Louie Mae account for the same verified email. We do not request access to your Gmail, contacts, calendar, Drive or any other Google data, and we do not post anything to Google on your behalf.

Louie Mae's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers listed below who host our site and accounts.

How we use information

  • To process quotes and orders, arrange delivery and send related emails.
  • To review and verify trade applications and show trade pricing to verified accounts.
  • To run the partner program: crediting partner links, calculating commission and referral credits, and paying partners.
  • To keep your projects, boards and inspiration in sync across your devices.
  • To prevent fraud, keep the site secure and meet legal and tax obligations.
  • To send marketing emails, only if you have agreed to receive them.

Who we share information with

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business, and only as needed for them to do that work:

  • Convex (database, file storage and sign-in), Vercel (website hosting), Stripe (payments and invoices), and Resend (transactional email).
  • Our furniture suppliers and delivery carriers, who receive the name, address and phone number needed to fulfil your order.
  • If you request a quote through a partner's link, that partner sees that a quote or order was made and its value, so their commission can be calculated. Partners do not see your contact or payment details.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of our customers.

Cookies and local storage

We use cookies and your browser's local storage to keep you signed in, remember your tray, boards and preferences, and remember a partner link for up to 30 days. You can clear these in your browser settings. Some features will not work without them.

How long we keep information

We keep account information while your account is open. Order, invoice, commission and tax records are kept as long as accounting and tax law requires. Trade application documents are kept while your trade account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards to resolve questions.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to close your account, by emailing withlove@louiemae.com. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time with the link in any email. If you signed in with Google, you can also remove Louie Mae's access from your Google Account settings. Residents of California and some other states may have additional rights under local law, including the right to know what we collect and to request deletion. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), store passwords only in hashed form, limit staff access to what is needed, and rely on established providers for payments and hosting. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

Louie Mae is not intended for children under 13, and House of Louie Mae is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy as our services change. We will post the new version here with a new "Last updated" date, and tell account holders by email about significant changes.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: House of Louie Mae LLC, Houston, Texas · withlove@louiemae.com.
