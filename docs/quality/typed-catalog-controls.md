# Typed HOLM catalog and saved-board controls

`CatalogProductCard`, `SaveBoardMenu`, `ProductPrice` and `formatHolmPrice` replace the corresponding rendering logic in the legacy HOLM owner. Their inputs are explicit product, board, price-mode and action contracts. Thin adapters retain the existing owner operations for product detail, saving, creating a board, Studio and the project tray. No price, discount, inventory or purchase authority moves into these components.

Cards and board actions preserve the selected source and variant. Saved covers still resolve through `useSavedBoardShelf`, independently of the current browse page. Missing saved products retain their records and unavailable presentation. A withdrawn source closes its picker and does not reopen it implicitly when availability recovers. Existing SVG icons, referral-aware Pinterest destinations, whole-dollar summary displays and exact single-item price displays remain.

The board picker is a labeled nonmodal dialog. It focuses search on opening and the name field when creating a board, associates validation errors with that field, and stays open if creation is rejected. Escape closes only the picker and returns focus to its explicit triggering button, including click-only activation inside the product dialog. Outside pointer/focus movement dismisses without stealing the destination focus. The owner supplies the trigger rather than assuming that pointer activation focused it. StrictMode effect replay preserves that return target.

The picker remains within the viewport with bounded scrolling. It moves to the other side of its trigger near viewport edges, and uses the available side for scrolling when a short landscape viewport cannot fit its full height. Pointer hit tests verify that the trigger remains reachable. Layout responds to viewport changes, scrolling and element resizing, with passive scroll listeners and cleanup. These are transient UI operations; trigger elements are never persisted in boards, projects or account records.

Validation includes typed component tests, exact variant identity and unavailable-state regressions, fifteen Chrome viewport/nested-dialog/card cases, including a short landscape viewport, and the actual HOLM owner/project fixture. The owner fixture verifies saved options outside the loaded catalog, quantities, independent variant prices, unavailable-item protections, and focus return inside the product modal. Browser fixtures use synthetic transport and local callbacks; they do not establish authenticated production isolation, supplier eligibility or successful payment.

This is one typed-boundary phase. Server-wide filtered/sorted discovery, larger room/editor/admin module extraction, complete authenticated persona coverage and the other outstanding specification gates remain separate work. Production acceptance requires final CI, review, exact preview and deployment verification.
