# Staged Zuo product review and publication

Staged products carry a product-level manual hold as well as variant-level holds. Resolving a variant hold alone does not release the product for publication. The catalog admin now has a paginated product review panel, explicit variant selection, a review note, individual stage transitions and explicit destination selection.

`catPublication.verifyCanonicalIdentity` addresses the import's mapping hold without requiring an already-published retail listing. For explicitly selected staged variants, it checks the canonical product, Zuo supplier, SKU, pack and draft variant identity. It records an idempotent same-product/same-variant mapping and resolves only `MAPPING_MISSING`. Conflicting or ambiguous existing mappings are rejected, and unrelated holds remain. The action does not publish a listing; referral links still require a published, eligible retail destination. Cross-product aliases continue through the separate reviewed mapping workflow.

`catPublication.releaseManualReview` requires an approver role, an included staged Zuo product and one to fifty distinct variants belonging to its exact staged SKU and pack. Archived products, excluded staging records and discontinued variants cannot be released through this action. It removes only the manual-review holds, then evaluates the remaining product gates and current sellability inside the same transaction. Missing costs, MAP, stock, delivery, images, rights and other unresolved holds cause the entire operation to roll back. The reason and reviewed variant IDs are audited. Prices, listings and checkout flags are not changed.

The action deliberately does not treat a note as source evidence or automatically clear other hold reasons. Non-staged imports need their own inclusion evidence workflow. Variants not selected remain held. Current pricing checks include the price's exact variant, channel and effective window; an expired approval is insufficient. Each selected publication channel rechecks its own price when the listing snapshot is created.

After manual review, the operator advances one stage at a time through the existing server gates. Publishing remains a separate action for named products and explicitly selected Louie Mae retail and/or HOLM trade destinations. The existing publication mutation commits both selected channels atomically or neither, and includes only variants that pass its gates. A published listing is not a claim that rollout flags or all operational dependencies have been enabled.

Changing the selected product resets local review selections, notes and destinations. Archived entries and exclusions other than temporary stock exclusions are disabled, discontinued variants cannot be selected, and errors are announced without a success message. The list supports additional pages rather than limiting work to the pipeline's first sample.

Tests cover anonymous rejection, incomplete-record rollback, current-price expiry, excluded/discontinued protection, valid review without publication, explicit variant/note requirements, explicit destinations, product-switch state reset and surfaced server errors. Production authenticated browser verification remains a separate acceptance step.

Run `node scripts/lighthouse/catalog-publication.mjs` after a production build for the isolated browser check. It uses the actual panel at 390, 844 and 1440 pixels with local query/mutation fixtures and network connections blocked, verifies explicit selections and errors, checks overflow and runs axe. It makes no production changes and does not substitute for authenticated production acceptance.

The shared purchase, estimate and new-referral eligibility gate also validates the approved revision against the exact variant, requested purchase channel, positive integer price and effective window. Expired or future approvals cannot be reused merely because their status still says approved. Historical paid-order snapshots are unchanged.

HOLM catalog, calculator and delivery paths pass the trade channel explicitly. The delivery and calculator paths can retain a valid trade offer when an optional retail comparison expires. Catalog cards that display a retail comparison still require that comparison to be current; do not display a stale reference price. Referral eligibility always requires the retail channel.

Draft image preparation allows a staged SKU excluded only for CURRENTLY_UNAVAILABLE, while retaining its stock exclusion and all sales holds. Exact SKU/pack, hash, host and discontinued checks remain. Empty, mixed or merchandising/discontinued exclusions are rejected. This lets reviewed photography be prepared before restocking without making the item purchasable.

`restoreStockExcludedDraft` restores only an initially imported draft excluded solely for `CURRENTLY_UNAVAILABLE`. An approver must select exact staged SKU/pack variants and document current, confirmed available-to-sell evidence. On-hand counts, expired revisions, discontinued variants, mixed exclusions and already-published products are rejected. Restoration retains/adds manual review holds, leaves the stage at imported draft, and does not change prices, listings or checkout flags. The action is audited; all normal qualification and publication steps remain mandatory.
