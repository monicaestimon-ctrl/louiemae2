# Supplier inquiry — House of Louie Mae

Hello [Name],

My name is [Name], and I represent House of Louie Mae, a U.S. furniture business sourcing pieces for homes, cafés, and commercial projects.

We are interested in [product link/model] in [finish/size], initially for approximately [quantity] pieces. We are evaluating this item for our catalog and customer quotations; this inquiry is not a purchase order or commitment to a minimum volume.

Please send the following information, preferably in a quotation/specification sheet:

1. Your company name, whether you are the manufacturer or a trading company, factory/pickup address, and contact details. Can you export to the U.S. directly or work with our nominated purchasing/export agent and freight forwarder?
2. Unit price and currency at your available quantity tiers; MOQ per model, color, finish and fabric; whether mixed variants are allowed; and whether a smaller first order is possible at a different unit price.
3. Available stock, standard production lead time, customization options, custom MOQ, sample cost, and any tooling or other setup charges.
4. Product dimensions, materials, finish options, net weight, care instructions, load rating and assembly instructions. Is the product designed and warranted for commercial café/restaurant use? Please attach supporting test reports or certificates where available.
5. Exact export-packed dimensions and gross weight for EACH carton or crate, the number of packages per item, and the quantity of items inside each package. Please include packaging photos and confirm whether packages are stackable. If palletized or crated, include those final dimensions and weights too.
6. What packaging is included, and what does stronger export packaging cost? Can you provide any applicable wood-packaging treatment documentation?
7. Quotation terms, including the Incoterm and named place, what is included/excluded, local transport costs if any, payment schedule, and quote validity.
8. Country of origin, your suggested HS code, and ability to provide a commercial invoice, packing list and applicable export/product documents. Our broker will confirm U.S. classification and import requirements.
9. Warranty terms, exclusions for commercial use, defect/missing-part policy, replacement options, and whether a pre-shipment inspection by our agent is permitted.
10. High-resolution product photos/videos and written permission for House of Louie Mae to use these materials on our website and in marketing.

Please also tell us whether you can label each carton with our SKU and project/order reference, and deliver to our consolidation warehouse in China or release the shipment to our forwarder.

We may initially purchase against confirmed customer projects, with potential repeat orders. Please distinguish confirmed information from estimates, especially packaging measurements and lead times.

Thank you,
