# Reviewed SKU economics and referral approvals

The finance diagnostics variant selector opens cost evidence and a separate pricing/referral review. The review shows supplier identity and unit basis, category, known cost/MAP, actual approved channel prices, published snapshot prices, approved eligible margin policies, current contribution scenarios, stock/freshness holds, visible tier rates and dated product exceptions. A legacy listing base is explicitly not labeled a final v2 price. Computation failures remain visible, not zero values or fallback commissions.

Only finance staff can read the economics. Approvers can assign a documented margin policy, approve a final merchandise price, explicitly enable/disable new referrals, and create a visible four-tier product-wide commission exception. Existing cohort/document constraints and exact money conversion remain authoritative. Trade savings come from actual approved prices; trade purchases earn no referral commission.

The form retains the originally reviewed values. Reactive changes freeze approval controls until an explicit refresh. Switching variants never submits another variant's old values. Every reviewed write carries a server-computed fingerprint covering variant identity, current costs/prices, relevant source observations, listings, category, approved-policy applicability, commission history and current calculations. The mutation recomputes that fingerprint in its transaction before making changes. Every approval endpoint requires its review hash; exception creation also requires the matching reviewed variant. Missing hashes cannot bypass review. Manual refresh changes the query input, and known future policy boundaries trigger a new query, with a one-minute fallback for other time-sensitive inputs. All writes retain existing actor/reason audit history and current economic validation.

Referral activation now strictly qualifies both supported suppliers with current evidence, in-stock availability, an approved retail price and the complete protected commission reserve. Failed activation rolls back state and success audit events. Unsupported supplier economics cannot enable referrals. A legacy Ashcroft base-price listing cannot generate v2 referral links merely because its canonical price was approved: the final-price offer must be published. Disabling new referrals does not rewrite earned commissions or historical snapshots. Dated exceptions preserve all four explicit tiers and historical protection; changing a rate cannot bypass ongoing price qualification.

Saving approval is not publishing or activation. The UI states when Product review and publication must republish final prices. Source evidence, stock, images, delivery configuration, checkout controls and program terms remain separate gates. No supplier costs, MAP terms or zero expenses are invented by this screen.

Acceptance covers unauthorized/read-only roles, stale-cost rejection, atomic failure of unfunded Ashcroft referral activation, exact-cent submissions, four-tier exceptions, stale-form refresh and variant switching. The isolated actual-component browser fixture runs in CI at320/844/1440px, checks keyboard submission, overflow, errors and axe, and blocks external requests. It makes no real financial or supplier calls.
